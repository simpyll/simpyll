# LipidPath limitations and release gate

**Independent qualified clinical review has not occurred. This is an educational preview, not clinically validated decision support.**

- Adult core coverage is partial guideline coverage. Children, HoFH drug algorithms, pregnancy/planning/lactation, dialysis initiation/continuation, complex cancer care, frailty/limited prognosis, detailed younger/older diabetes guidance, FCS drug selection and statin rechallenge/nonstatin symptom algorithms are deferred. Known HeFH without severe untreated LDL documentation is referred instead of using general-population risk.
- Contraindication/interaction review is user-confirmed, not performed by the app. No medication contraindication database, prescribing orders, specific statin selection or dose calculator exists. A confirmation cannot establish individual safety.
- ASCVD diagnosis and distinct major-event/high-risk-condition counts are clinician-established inputs. The tool does not infer ASCVD, FH, CKD chronicity, FCS or statin intolerance from isolated values. Count ACS and MI from the same event once. Age >65 is a high-risk condition, not ≥65. Major events and risk conditions must both be known before lower-risk secondary classification.
- Risk entry is manual and unverified externally. No official equation is reproduced, approximated or replaced with PCE. Do not enter a total-CVD or heart-failure result. After any other clinical edit, confirmation resets. Age 30–59 is required for 30-year estimates. Disease-specific diabetes/CKD/HIV risk-based intensification beyond the implemented branches is deferred.
- LDL is reported by the laboratory, not calculated here. ApoB-only/non-HDL-only severe untreated phenotypes trigger review rather than silently entering ordinary primary prevention. Severe-LDL guideline heading/footnote differences are documented in the source map.
- Missing values are not negatives. A current low LDL does not disprove severe untreated history. Baseline percentage change is unknown without untreated baseline. Missing non-HDL or percent change means overall goal achievement cannot be concluded from LDL alone.
- CAC-zero deferral is narrow and conditional, never a blanket de-risking statement. Sex/age and percentile restrictions matter. CAC ≥300 may support optional tighter targets; these are not substituted for standard targets. CAC is not clinical ASCVD.
- Persistence of high TG requires clinical confirmation of fasting elevation after secondary-cause management, lifestyle trial and stable therapy if indicated. A severe value prompts review without diagnosing pancreatitis. TG ≥1000 shifts priority toward pancreatitis prevention; IPE is a restricted residual-ASCVD-risk discussion, not a class-wide omega-3 claim.
- Lp(a) unit-specific thresholds are supported without universal conversion or automatic risk multiplier. ApoB assessment is supported without automatic goals. Inclisiran efficacy is distinguished from pending outcome evidence in the source guideline.
- The tool does not capture every comorbidity, recommendation, risk enhancer or exception. Severe symptoms or acute clinical illness require professional evaluation outside this tool. No institution or guideline organization has endorsed or certified the app.

Release must wait for qualified independent review of every shipped clinical branch and explicit owner review. Pending/failed browser checks must be recorded accurately. Keep the implementation PR unmerged until these release requirements are resolved.
