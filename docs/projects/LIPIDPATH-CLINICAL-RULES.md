Evidence registry is machine-readable TypeScript. Each emitted recommendation/goal points to a stable recommendation-specific entry. Trace links the entered eligibility facts, output, evidence and scenario IDs. Numerical/lab validation is technical verification, not clinical validation. No independent qualified clinical review has occurred; owner must arrange it before deployment. Adult core coverage is intentionally distinct from comprehensive guideline coverage.

Apparent source differences preserved: the severe-LDL footnote also mentions non-HDL/apoB criteria, while the recommendation headings use LDL ≥190. Those alternate-entry phenotypes are referred for review, not classified as ordinary primary prevention. The secondary-prevention table R5 is COR 1/A; do not take the older summary table's grade instead. Age >65 in Fig 10 is a strict boundary. Optional tighter targets remain optional and do not replace standard goals.

## Partial assessment and sample timing

The partial assessment separately records reported ASCVD, diabetes, triglyceride elevation and a documented or possible severe LDL history. These observations do not establish a complete treatment pathway, target, diagnosis or medication choice. They remain visible when unrelated questions or treatment-safety review are incomplete; unsupported-population warnings continue to apply. A source-linked discussion of statin treatment is available only when its relevant documented indication and the implemented adult scope, safety and symptom guards are satisfied. The existing full pathway remains responsible for targets and intensification. Independent qualified clinical review of this distinction is required before release.

A current measurement is considered untreated for severe-LDL classification only when the user reports that it was taken before any cholesterol-lowering treatment. The absence of a current statin alone no longer establishes an untreated measurement. Current medicine use is a separate answer. A current LDL ≥190 after treatment or with unknown timing is identified as a finding requiring clarification; it does not independently establish untreated history. This is a clinical rule change, distinct from the patient-language and workflow changes. Source: §4.2.4.3 R1–2, e1198 and Figure 7; see the source map above. The cutoff is evaluated in normalized mg/dL without rounding; displayed mmol/L equivalents in source material are rounded.

The risk entry remains manual and uses the official AHA PREVENT-ASCVD outcome. The displayed reasons describe eligibility for LipidPath's treatment pathway, not a complete statement of the AHA calculator's eligibility. Changing an entered risk value or other profile field clears its confirmation. A missing risk score is never interpreted as low risk.

When unrelated information is missing, a secondary-prevention goal may be displayed only after the shared scope/safety guard, known event counts, and explicit absence of CKD, FH and severe LDL history, with current LDL below 190. These extra guards prevent an incomplete overlapping indication from making a less stringent target appear definitive. The classification and goals use §4.2.6 and Figures 10–12. When these prerequisites are not established, only the reported finding and missing questions are shown.

Partial observations also identify reported positive coronary calcium and elevated Lp(a), without assigning a calcium target from an absent percentile or converting Lp(a) units. A CAC score of 1–99 with no percentile prompts for that missing detail when it is relevant. These observations are educational; eligibility and treatment goals remain subject to the source-mapped full pathways.

Known unsupported age or reproductive context, homozygous FH, dialysis, complex-care status, or an explicitly uncompleted safety review is disclosed even if other fields are missing; the entered findings remain available, but automated goals and medication selection are withheld. The general risk-based treatment branch requires diabetes status to be explicitly negative, consistent with the engine's separate diabetes branch; this is not a statement of the official calculator's independent eligibility.

Medication-specific discussions and intensification in the full engine no longer treat unanswered statin-associated symptoms as absent. When a current statin is reported, an explicit negative symptom answer is required for these automated discussions; an unknown answer is listed as missing. No current statin is handled separately. The finding and pathway summaries remain available. This is a conservative software safety guard, not a claim that missing symptoms establish intolerance.
